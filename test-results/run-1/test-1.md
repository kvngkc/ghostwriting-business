**Q3. What are your favourite interview questions? Give them to me word for word, as you'd say them on a call.**

**Q4. When a client gives you a vague answer, how do you dig for the specifics?**

**Q5. What are your go-to post formats? For each, tell me how it works and paste one real post you wrote in that format.**
