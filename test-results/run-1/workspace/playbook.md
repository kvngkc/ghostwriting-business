# Studio Playbook

This is how this ghostwriter works. Every mode follows it over the skill's defaults.

## Who I write for
- Client types: founders of small service businesses, mostly hospitality and professional services
- Platforms I deliver: LinkedIn only
- Posts per client per month: 8

## My process, in order
1. 45-minute onboarding call
2. 30-minute monthly call, recorded
3. Drafts in a Google Doc every Monday
4. Client comments by Thursday
5. I schedule on Friday

## How I interview
- My favourite questions (verbatim):
  - "What did a client say this month that stuck with you?"
  - "What did you disagree with this month?"
  - "What number surprised you?"
- How I dig for specifics: I always ask for the number, and I always ask "what happened next?"

## How I structure posts
- My go-to formats (no example posts supplied yet: [ASK WRITER: one real post per format]):
  - **The lesson from a moment:** one specific scene, then the one thing it taught them. Short lines.
  - **The list of three:** three things they check or believe, with the third being the surprising one.
  - **The contrarian:** a common belief, why they disagree, and what they do instead.
- How I write hooks: first line under 12 words, concrete, no questions as openers. (No example hooks supplied yet.)
- How I end posts: one short line that a reader could repeat. No "What do you think?" endings.
- Length: 60–150 words.

## My editing rules
- Always: one idea per post. Line break every one or two sentences.
- Never: hashtags.
- Banned words: "journey", "passionate", "excited to announce", "game-changer", "leverage".
