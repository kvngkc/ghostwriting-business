## D1. Lesson from a moment: that's counting (S2)

That's not accounting, that's counting.

I say it all the time. My team are sick of it.

One of our new bakery clients was binning about 18% of what they baked.

They didn't know, because nobody counted the bin.

We set them up with a sheet. Literally a clipboard by the back door.

Six weeks later they were at 9%.

But it shows up in the accounts.

## D2. Contrarian: "just the bookkeepers" (S1)

A client called us "just the bookkeepers" in September.

We'd told them part of their business was losing about £2,000 a month. Eleven months in, they were still calling it "ramping up".

Two weeks later they moved to another firm.

Lovely people. I don't regret it.

If I'd softened it, they'd have kept bleeding money.

And I'd have been complicit.

## D3. Statement post, switched from list of three: the menu test (S6)

Restaurant owners get into the trade because they love feeding people.

Nobody opens a restaurant because they love spreadsheets.

So my job is to make the numbers so simple they don't feel like homework.

If my report takes longer to read than a menu, I've failed.

## D5. Contrarian: cloud software (S4)

I've changed my mind about cloud software.

Five years ago I'd have told every client to move everything to the cloud tomorrow.

Now I've seen people with beautiful dashboards who haven't opened them since March.

The software matters less than whether someone actually looks at the numbers.
