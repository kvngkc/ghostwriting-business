# Client Onboarding Checklist

Copy this list into the client's row in the Clients tab notes, or tick it off here.

## Day 0: signed and paid
- [ ] Agreement signed by both parties
- [ ] First invoice paid
- [ ] Client moved to Won in the pipeline and added in the Clients tab
- [ ] Private client folder created: `clients/<slug>/` with `samples/` and `transcripts/`
- [ ] Welcome email sent with: what happens next, interview booking link, what to send you

## Days 1–3: material
- [ ] Client sends 10+ pieces of their own writing (posts, emails, newsletters, talk transcripts)
- [ ] Client confirms which pieces they wrote themselves
- [ ] Onboarding interview booked (45 minutes) and recorded with permission
- [ ] Client says which topics are off limits, which clients and numbers may be named, and their disclosure preference

## Days 3–7: voice
- [ ] Interview transcribed into `transcripts/`
- [ ] Voice profile built with the `voice-profile` skill (Build mode)
- [ ] `stories.md` filled from the transcript
- [ ] 3 test posts sent; client corrections recorded; profile marked approved

## Days 7–10: publishing setup
- [ ] Client connects LinkedIn to the scheduler through official login (no passwords)
- [ ] Posting days and times agreed
- [ ] Approval method agreed (shared doc, email or Notion), with the [3]-day review window
- [ ] First 2 weeks of posts drafted, edited, approved and scheduled

## Ongoing
- [ ] Monthly interview booked as a recurring event
- [ ] Monthly report date set
- [ ] Next invoice date set in the tracker
