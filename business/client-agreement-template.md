# Ghostwriting Services Agreement (template)

> **Before you use this:** this template is a starting point, not legal advice. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review it once, and again before using it with clients in a new country.

**This agreement** is between **[Your business name]**, registered in [country] ([registration number]), of [address] ("Writer"), and **[Client legal name]** of [address] ("Client"). It starts on [date].

## 1. Services
1.1 Each month the Writer will: hold one interview call of up to [45] minutes; draft [N] LinkedIn posts in the Client's voice; deliver drafts for approval weekly; schedule approved posts if the Client grants scheduling access; and send a short monthly report.
1.2 Each post includes up to [2] rounds of revisions. Additional work (other platforms, newsletters, articles) needs written agreement and is priced separately.
1.3 The Client will attend the monthly interview, review drafts within [3] business days, and provide accurate information.

## 2. Approval and responsibility
2.1 Nothing is published without the Client's approval. Approval may be given by message or email.
2.2 Content is based on information the Client provides. The Writer won't knowingly add facts, figures, names or claims the Client hasn't supplied.
2.3 The Client is responsible for the accuracy of approved content and for its compliance with laws and platform rules that apply to the Client.

## 3. Fees and payment
3.1 Fee: [currency] [amount] per month, invoiced monthly in advance and due within [7] days.
3.2 If an invoice is more than [14] days overdue, the Writer may pause work until it is paid. Unused posts don't carry over beyond [one] month.
3.3 Fees exclude taxes and transfer charges, which are paid by [Client/each party for its own charges].

## 4. Term and termination
4.1 Initial term of [3] months, then month to month.
4.2 After the initial term, either party may end this agreement with [30] days' written notice.
4.3 Either party may end it immediately if the other materially breaches it and doesn't fix the breach within [14] days of notice.
4.4 Fees already paid aren't refunded, except for a month in which no drafts were delivered.

## 5. Ownership
5.1 Once the relevant fees are paid, the Client owns all rights in the final approved content, and the Writer assigns any copyright in it to the Client.
5.2 The Writer keeps ownership of its own methods, templates, prompts, tools and the voice profile format. The Client may receive a copy of its own voice profile on request when the agreement ends.
5.3 The Writer won't claim public authorship of the content. The Writer may name the Client as a client in its marketing only with the Client's written permission.

## 6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party will keep the other's confidential information private and use it only for this agreement. This continues for [2] years after the agreement ends.
6.2 Interview recordings, transcripts, drafts and business information from the Client are confidential.

## 7. Use of AI tools
7.1 The Writer uses AI writing tools to help with drafting and editing. A human reviews and edits all content, and the Client approves all content before publication.
7.2 The Writer will only use AI services whose terms don't permit training on the Client's inputs, and won't put Client material into public or consumer AI tools that do.
7.3 Whether to disclose the use of a writer or AI tools publicly is the Client's decision, subject to any platform rules that apply.

## 8. Data protection
8.1 The Writer processes the Client's personal data only to provide the services, in line with applicable data protection law (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where relevant, UK/EU GDPR).
8.2 The Writer stores Client material in [location/tool], protected by access controls and two-factor authentication.
8.3 Within [30] days after the agreement ends, the Writer will delete or return Client material, except records it must keep by law (such as invoices).

## 9. Account access
9.1 If the Client connects a social media account to the Writer's scheduling tool, it does so through the platform's official login. The Writer will never ask for the Client's password.
9.2 The Client may revoke access at any time. The Writer won't use automation that breaks a platform's terms on the Client's account.

## 10. Liability
10.1 The Writer doesn't guarantee specific results such as followers, engagement or revenue.
10.2 Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the [3] months before the claim, except for breach of confidentiality or where the law doesn't allow a limit.

## 11. General
11.1 Governing law: [jurisdiction]. Disputes: [courts / arbitration venue].
11.2 This is the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing.
11.3 Neither party may transfer this agreement without the other's consent.
