# Sales Pipeline Playbook (full cycle)

Each stage below matches a column in the **Ghostwriting HQ** tracker (Pipeline tab). A deal moves forward only when the exit condition for its stage is met.

| # | Stage | Exit condition | Target time in stage |
|---|-------|----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Prospect | Researched; one true, specific hook written down | — |
| 2 | Contacted | Email 1 or LinkedIn connect sent | 21 days max, then Lost (no reply) |
| 3 | Replied | Positive reply received | 24h to respond |
| 4 | Sample sent | 3 sample posts delivered | 3 days |
| 5 | Call booked | Discovery call on the calendar | — |
| 6 | Proposal sent | Proposal and agreement sent | 7 days, one follow-up |
| 7 | Won | Agreement signed and first invoice paid | — |
| 8 | Lost | Reason recorded | — |

Won deals move into the **Clients** tab.

---

## Stage 1. Prospect: who to target

Ideal client (adjust once you have data):
- Founder, managing partner, consultant or coach selling a high-value B2B service. One new client is worth much more to them than your fee.
- Has real expertise and material to draw on: talks, podcasts, newsletters, client stories.
- Has a LinkedIn presence but posts rarely, or posts generic content.
- Can pay in a currency you can receive (see `business/business-setup.md`).

Where to find them: LinkedIn search, podcast guest lists in your niche, conference speaker pages, newsletter authors, your own network. Record each one in the tracker with the hook.

Daily target to start: 10 new prospects researched, 10 contacted.

## Stage 2–4. Outreach and sample

Use `sales/outreach-sequences.md`. The free sample is the main conversion step, so make it good: 3 posts, only from their own material, with your best edit.

## Stage 5. Discovery call (20–30 minutes)

Goal: find out whether they have a real problem you can solve and whether they can pay. If not, end the call politely.

1. **Context (5 min):** "What made you reply?" "What's LinkedIn supposed to do for your business?"
2. **Current state (5 min):** "How often do you post now? What gets in the way?" "Have you tried a writer or AI tools before? What happened?"
3. **Stakes (5 min):** "What's one new client worth to you?" "If you posted three times a week for six months, what would change?"
4. **Fit (5 min):** explain the process: one monthly interview, drafts every week, 10 minutes of approvals, posts scheduled for them.
5. **Close the call:** "Based on this, I'd suggest [tier]. I'll send a one-page proposal today. Can we look at it together on [day]?"

Write the answers into the deal's notes. Use their exact words in the proposal.

### Common objections

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "It won't sound like me." | "That's why it starts with an interview and a voice profile, and nothing goes out without your approval. You saw the samples. What would you change?" |
| "Too expensive." | "Compared with what one new client is worth to you, [their number from the call], what would it need to bring in to pay for itself?" Then offer a smaller tier, never a discount on the same scope. |
| "I can do it with ChatGPT." | "You can. The hard part is the hours of interviewing, picking the right story and editing. That's what you'd be buying back." |
| "Is it dishonest?" | "The ideas, stories and opinions are all yours. I shape them. You approve every word. Many founders work with writers, and you decide whether to mention it." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to decide yes?" Book a follow-up date. |

## Stage 6. Proposal

Send within 24 hours of the call. One page, using their words.

```
Proposal for [Client name]
Prepared [date], valid for 14 days

What you told me
- Goal: [their words]
- What's in the way: [their words]
- What a new client is worth: [their number]

What I'll do each month
- One 30–45 minute interview call
- [N] LinkedIn posts in your voice, drafted from your own stories and opinions
- Weekly approval doc; you approve or edit every post before it goes out
- Scheduling at your best times
- Monthly one-page report: posts, reach, engagement, inbound messages

Your part: one call a month and about 10 minutes a week to approve.

Investment: [tier name], [price] per month, billed monthly in advance.
Minimum term: [3] months, then month to month with 30 days' notice.

Next step: sign the agreement and pay the first invoice. We'll book the onboarding interview within 3 days.
```

Pricing: set your tiers with the `pricing` and `offers` / `hormozi-framework` skills before sending your first proposal. This playbook doesn't set prices for you.

## Stage 7. Close and onboard

1. Send the client agreement (`business/client-agreement-template.md`) and the first invoice together.
2. Once paid, run `business/client-onboarding-checklist.md`.
3. Move the deal to Won and create the client in the Clients tab.

## Stage 8. Lost

Always record why: no reply, no budget, bad timing, chose another provider, not a fit. Review lost reasons every month. "Not now" deals get a check-back date.

---

## Retention and expansion (after the sale)

- **Monthly:** interview, update the voice profile (Update mode), report.
- **Month 2:** ask for a testimonial once they've seen a few posts perform.
- **Month 3:** renewal check: "What's working, what should change?" Raise rates for new clients, not existing ones, until they renew.
- **Referrals:** after a good month, ask "Who else do you know who should be posting but isn't?" Consider a referral thank-you of one free month.
- **Upsells, only if you can deliver them:** newsletter, repurposing into X/Twitter threads, a second executive at the same company.

## Weekly metrics (from the tracker)

| Metric | Healthy early target (adjust to your data) |
|--------|-------------------------------------------|
| New prospects contacted | 50 per week |
| Positive reply rate | 5–10% |
| Sample → call | 40%+ |
| Call → won | 25%+ |
| Client churn | under 1 in 10 per month |
