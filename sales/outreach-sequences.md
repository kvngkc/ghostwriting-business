# Outreach Sequences

Built with the `cold-email` skill (value-first teardown play, 4-touch follow-up cadence). Fill every `[bracket]` by hand from real research on that person. If you cannot fill a bracket with something true and specific, skip the prospect.

Before sending anything, see "Sending setup" at the bottom.

---

## Sequence A: Cold email, "free sample" play

Target: founders, consultants and agency owners who have expertise but post rarely or inconsistently on LinkedIn.

**Email 1 (day 0)** — asks permission, no link
> Subject: your linkedin
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> I listened to your [podcast episode / talk / webinar] on [topic]. The part about [specific point they made] was sharper than anything on your LinkedIn, which has had [N] posts since [month].
>
> I'm a ghostwriter for [type of person, e.g. B2B founders]. I turned that one point into 3 short posts in your voice. Want me to send them over?
>
> [Your name]
>
> [Postal address] · Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

**If they say yes:** send the 3 posts in a Google Doc within 24 hours. Use only what they said in the source. Ask one question: "Which of these sounds most like you?"

**Email 2 (day 3)** — new angle
> Subject: re-using what you've already said
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Most of the people I write for already have months of good material in calls, talks and client emails. The writing part is turning that into posts they'd actually sign.
>
> Happy to show you what that looks like using your [episode / article] as the source. Useful?
>
> [Your name]

**Email 3 (day 8)** — proof
> Subject: [peer's role] example
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> [Real client or your own account] went from [real starting point] to [real result] in [time] with one 30-minute call a month and about 10 minutes of approvals a week.
>
> If that kind of setup would help you, I can send the 3 sample posts I mentioned.
>
> [Your name]

Use only results you can show. Before your first client, use your own LinkedIn numbers.

**Email 4 (day 21)** — breakup
> Subject: closing the loop
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> I'll assume LinkedIn isn't a priority right now, so this is my last note. If it becomes one, the 3 posts from your [source] are yours to keep. Just reply and I'll send them.
>
> Good luck with [their current goal].
>
> [Your name]

## Sequence B: LinkedIn (manual only)

Do this by hand. Tools that automate connection requests or messages break LinkedIn's User Agreement and put your account at risk.

1. **Days 1–5:** follow them and leave 2–3 real comments on their posts (add a point; never just "great post").
2. **Connection request (no pitch):**
   > Hi [first name], I've enjoyed your posts on [topic], especially the one about [specific post]. Would be good to connect.
3. **After they accept, wait 2 days, then:**
   > Thanks for connecting. Your point about [X] in [post/talk] deserved more reach. I write LinkedIn posts for [type of person] from their own calls and talks. Want me to turn that point into a couple of sample posts for you, no strings?
4. **If they say yes:** same as Sequence A, deliver in 24 hours.
5. **If no reply in 7 days:** one more message with something useful (a post idea pulled from their own content). Then stop.

Stop every channel the moment they reply on any of them.

## Sequence C: Warm network

For people who already know you. Send to 20–30 people in week one.

> Hi [name], I've started a ghostwriting service for [type of person]: I interview them once a month and turn it into LinkedIn posts in their voice. Do you know anyone who has expertise but never finds time to post? I'm taking on [N] founding clients at a reduced rate in exchange for a testimonial.

## Reply handling

| Reply | What to send |
|-------|--------------|
| Yes / curious | Deliver samples in 24h. Then: "Want to talk through how the monthly setup works? Here are two times: [time 1], [time 2], or pick one here: [booking link]." |
| "How much?" | "It depends on volume. Most clients are on [tier name] at [your price]. Worth a 20-minute call to see if it fits before you decide?" |
| "I use AI already" | "Makes sense. What I add is the interview: the posts come from your stories and opinions, and I edit each one by hand so it sounds like you. Want to see a side-by-side?" |
| "Not now" | "No problem. Mind if I check back in [month]?" Log the date in the tracker. |
| Referral | Thank them, ask permission to mention their name, email the referral within 24h. |
| Unsubscribe / no | "Understood, I've removed you." Mark them do-not-contact in the tracker, and never email them again. |

## Sending setup (do this before email 1)

- Buy a separate domain for outreach (e.g. `get[yourbrand].com`). Never send cold email from your main domain.
- Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on it, then warm the mailbox for 3–4 weeks before cold sends.
- Verify every address before sending. Keep bounces under 2%.
- Turn off open and click tracking. Count replies.
- Plain text, one link at most, no attachments.
- Put a postal address and a plain opt-out line in every email (required under CAN-SPAM for US recipients).
- Start at 20–30 new emails a day per mailbox.
- **UK/EU:** cold email to company addresses is generally allowed under legitimate interest if it's relevant to their role and has an opt-out. UK sole traders count as individuals and need consent. Skip them, or reach them on LinkedIn instead.
