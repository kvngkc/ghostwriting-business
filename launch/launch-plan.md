# Launch Plan: Solo Ghostwriters, Free

**Goal for the first 6 weeks:** 10 solo ghostwriters using Ghostwriter Studio on real client work, and 5 of them telling you in detail what saved time and what didn't. Don't measure downloads. Measure use and feedback.

## Who

Solo ghostwriters writing LinkedIn posts (or newsletters) for 2–10 clients. They feel the pain most: several voices to keep straight, transcripts to dig through, no team to hand work to.

Signs someone is a good fit:
- Says "ghostwriter" or "I write for founders/executives" in their headline
- Posts about their process, client calls or workload
- Has more than one client (look for "my clients" posts or case studies)

## Week 1: test it yourself
- [ ] Install the skill on claude.ai from `dist/ghostwriter-studio.zip` and run every mode once
- [ ] Use a real transcript if you have one (yours or a willing friend's). Otherwise record a 20-minute mock client interview with someone and use that.
- [ ] Note anything confusing, slow or wrong. Fix the skill and rebuild the zip.
- [ ] Write your own Studio Playbook with it. You'll need it to demo the skill.

## Weeks 2–3: 5 hand-picked testers (before the public post)
- [ ] Find 20 solo ghostwriters on LinkedIn. Add each to the tracker's Outreach tab with what you noticed about them.
- [ ] Message them by hand, one at a time. Keep it short, no link in the first message:
  > Hi [name], saw your post about [specific thing]. I built a free Claude skill for ghostwriters that runs your own process: mining call transcripts, keeping client voices straight, repurposing. I'm looking for 5 people to try it on real client work and tell me what's broken. Interested?
- [ ] For each "yes": send the zip and the install steps, and offer a 15-minute call to set up their playbook together.
- [ ] After a week of use, ask the feedback questions below. Log the answers in the tracker's Users tab.

## Week 4: fix and launch publicly
- [ ] Fix the top 3 problems testers raised.
- [ ] Ask the happiest testers whether you can quote them.
- [ ] Publish the LinkedIn launch post (`launch-post.md` + slides). Add a real tester quote if you have one.
- [ ] Reply to every "studio" comment within a few hours: DM the zip and install steps.

## Weeks 5–6: follow up
- [ ] Message everyone who received it, 7 days later: "Did you get it installed? What did you try first?"
- [ ] Ask active users the feedback questions.
- [ ] Decide what to build next, based on what people actually used.

## Feedback questions (ask the same ones every time)
1. Which mode did you use most? Which did you never touch?
2. What did it save you time on? Roughly how much per client per week?
3. Where did it get the client's voice wrong?
4. Did it ever invent something or ignore your playbook?
5. What would make you recommend it to another ghostwriter?
6. If it stopped being free, would you pay for it? What would it need to do first?

Question 6 is how you'll find out whether there's a paid version later, without having to decide now.

## Don't
- Don't use automated LinkedIn DMs or connection tools. Message by hand.
- Don't promise time savings you haven't measured. Quote testers' own numbers once you have them.
- Don't share testers' client material anywhere. The skill keeps it in their own Claude account.
