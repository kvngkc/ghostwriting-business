I'm looking for 10 ghostwriters to break something I built.

It's free. I just need people who'll be honest with me.

The problem I kept seeing is that most of a ghostwriter's week isn't writing.

It's digging through a 45-minute transcript for the one story worth telling.
It's remembering which client says "folks" and which one would rather quit LinkedIn.
It's checking you haven't already used that anecdote.
It's turning one approved post into a caption, a thread and a reel script.

So I built Ghostwriter Studio.

It's a free Claude skill that learns YOUR process (your formats, your hooks, your banned words) and runs it for every client:

→ Mines call transcripts for stories, with the exact line each one came from
→ Builds a voice profile with a real quote behind every rule
→ Preps your next client call
→ Drafts in your structure and your client's voice
→ Checks any draft line by line: "does this sound like them?"
→ Repurposes for X, Instagram, Facebook and short video
→ Writes the monthly client report from your numbers

You stay the writer. It takes the slow parts.

And it never makes up a story, a number or a name. If something's missing, it asks.

I've run it end to end on a test client. Now I need real ghostwriters with real clients to show me where it falls over.

What you get:
✓ The skill, free
✓ A 15-minute call to set up your playbook together
✓ A say in what gets built next

What I ask:
✓ Use it on real client work for a week
✓ Tell me honestly what's broken

10 spots.

Comment "studio" and I'll message you.

Know a ghostwriter who's buried in transcripts? Tag them below.

P.S. My favourite test: I asked it to write about a client's growth numbers that didn't exist. It refused and asked me for them instead. That's the standard I want it held to.
