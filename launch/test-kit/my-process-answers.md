# Sample answers for "Set up my studio" (FICTIONAL GHOSTWRITER)

Use these if you want to test the setup interview without describing your own process yet. Paste the relevant answer when the skill asks each question. Better still, answer with your real process.

- **Who I write for:** founders of small service businesses, mostly hospitality and professional services. LinkedIn only. 8 posts per client per month.
- **My process:** 45-minute onboarding call. Then a 30-minute monthly call, recorded. Drafts in a Google Doc every Monday, client comments by Thursday, I schedule on Friday.
- **Favourite interview questions:** "What did a client say this month that stuck with you?" / "What did you disagree with this month?" / "What number surprised you?"
- **How I dig:** I always ask for the number, and I always ask "what happened next?"
- **Formats I use:**
  - *The lesson from a moment:* one specific scene, then the one thing it taught them. Short lines.
  - *The list of three:* three things they check or believe, with the third being the surprising one.
  - *The contrarian:* a common belief, why they disagree, and what they do instead.
- **Hooks:** first line under 12 words, concrete, no questions as openers.
- **Endings:** one short line that a reader could repeat. No "What do you think?" endings.
- **Length:** 60–150 words.
- **Editing rules:** one idea per post. Line break every one or two sentences. No hashtags.
- **Banned words:** "journey", "passionate", "excited to announce", "game-changer", "leverage".
- **Approvals:** Google Doc, one revision round.
- **Reporting:** monthly: posts published, best post by comments, profile views, inbound messages, next month's plan.
