# Leaflet Test Plan

**What you're testing:** whether a heritage firm owner understands the offer quickly, believes it, and wants the free trial. You're also testing which headline works better.

- **A:** "Your best work, seen."
- **B:** "Architects should know your name."

The leaflet: https://claude.ai/artifact/EqTLEKaLPAoAifjjqSUv2n (two artboards, A and B). Export each as a PDF from the canvas (Share › Export). Use the PDF on a phone screen, email or WhatsApp, or print it.

Before testing, replace the placeholders: [Your name], WhatsApp [number], [email], [website].

---

## Round 1: a quick check with anyone (1–2 days)

Show it to 3–5 people who don't know the business, ideally people who run a trade or small firm. This catches confusing wording before you use up real prospects.

1. **5-second test.** Show the leaflet for 5 seconds, then hide it. Ask:
   - "What is this offering?"
   - "Who is it for?"
   - Pass: they say something like "someone writes social media posts for builders/craftspeople". Fail: "a building firm", "a marketing agency", "not sure".
2. **Read-through.** Let them read it fully, then ask:
   - "What would you have to do each week?" (Pass: "send voice notes/photos and approve")
   - "What would make you not trust this?"
   - "Which word or line confused you?"
3. Fix anything that two or more people stumble on.

## Round 2: real owners (2–3 weeks)

Use the 10 owner conversations from the niche validation plan (`niche-research.md`, section 6). Show half of them A and half B. Alternate, so the first person sees A, the second B, and so on.

**On a call or in person**
1. Send the PDF on WhatsApp or show it. Say: "I'm testing a leaflet. Can I ask you three quick questions about it?"
2. Run the 5-second test (same questions as round 1).
3. Ask:
   - "In your words, what do I do?"
   - "Is there anything here you don't believe?"
   - "Would you send me one job to try it? Why or why not?"
4. Don't explain or defend. Write down their exact words.

**By email (for the cold list)**
Send A to half the list and B to the other half, as the email-1 attachment or link after they reply "yes" to samples. Track replies per version in the tracker (put "Leaflet A" or "Leaflet B" in each deal's notes).

## Scoring sheet

Copy into a spreadsheet or the deal notes, one row per person.

## How to decide

| Result | What it means | Do this |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| 4 of 5 understand it in 5 seconds | The message is clear | Keep it |
| Fewer than 3 of 5 understand it | The headline or subhead is unclear | Rewrite the top section, test again |
| Same doubt raised 3+ times | A trust gap | Answer it on the leaflet, e.g. a sample post, a testimonial, where you're based |
| One version gets clearly more "yes, I'd try it" | That headline wins | Use it on the leaflet, website and email 1 |
| No clear winner after 10 | Both are fine | Keep A (shorter), move on |
| Nobody would try it, even free | The offer is the problem, not the leaflet | Revisit the niche (go/no-go in niche-research.md) |

Ten people won't give you statistically solid numbers. Look for the same comment coming up again and again, and listen to people's exact words. That's what to act on.

## After the test

- Update the leaflet with the winning headline and any fixes (ask Claude to edit the canvas).
- Reuse the exact phrases owners used in your outreach emails and posts.
- Log the result in the tracker's Launch tab.
